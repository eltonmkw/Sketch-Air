Draw in mid-air with your hand and webcam. Turn your sketch into AI artwork.

## Open Sketch Air

**[Launch Sketch Air](https://play-sketch-air.vercel.app)** — no installation needed.

Bring a desktop browser, webcam, and internet connection. Sketch freely; connect your Gemini key to generate AI artwork from your air sketch.

## Create your first artwork

1. Close the key dialog with **X** or **Escape** to draw first.
2. Click **Start** and allow camera access.
3. Pinch thumb and index finger, then move to draw. Release to stop.
4. Describe your idea in **Describe object...** or use the microphone.
5. Choose a style and click **Transform**. Connect your Gemini key if prompted, then retry.
6. Try another style beneath the result, or close it with **X**.

Choose colors and brush size in the toolbar. Use **Undo** or **Clear** for corrections.

## Hand gestures

| Gesture | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Hold a pinch | Draw |
| Thumbs up | Stop the stroke |
| One finger | Toggle voice input |
| Two fingers | Transform |
| Three fingers, result visible | Save to gallery |
| Fast swipe | Erase |

Hold gestures steady; pause between actions. **Drawing: ON/OFF** pauses drawing. **Stop** turns off the camera and clears your sketch.

## Save your artwork

- **Save File:** download the visible result as a PNG.
- **Three fingers:** save to the gallery; download or delete from there.
- **Gallery:** show or hide saved results.

**Download before leaving. Refreshing or closing the page clears the gallery.**

## Connect or remove your Gemini key

Open **API key** → enter your [Gemini key](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) → **Test Connection** → **Done**.

- **Storage:** clears on refresh. **Remember on this device** saves it as plaintext for 30 days.
- **Google:** receives your key, sketch, and description. Generation and style changes may cost money; a connection test does not guarantee quota or billing access.
- **Removal:** use **Remove saved key**. If removal fails, clear the site's browser data. This does not revoke the key at Google.

## Need help?

- **Camera unavailable:** Allow access, close other camera apps, then retry Start.
- **Stuck loading:** Check your connection and blocked resources, then reload.
- **No microphone:** Type instead; voice support varies by browser.
- **Generation failed:** Check the error, key restrictions, image-generation access, quota, and billing.
